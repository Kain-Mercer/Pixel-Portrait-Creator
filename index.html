The face plate. Rows 22–40 have the same width on every head shape. Brows, eyes, nose and mouth only ever draw inside that band, so a shape swap can never push a feature off the face. Silhouette variety lives in the crown and the jaw.

Ramps, not colors. Each material is a 5–7 tone ramp built in OKLCH from one base color. Shadows drift toward red or violet and highlights toward gold. Pixels store a ramp and a tone index, so recoloring a whole character is a palette swap, and shadows can be applied after the fact.

Light from the upper left. Heads, necks, torsos and hair locks shade from surface normals, and front hair casts a shadow down and to the right. Use Copy recipe to get the JSON that reproduces a character exactly.
